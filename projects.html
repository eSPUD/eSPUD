pi-hub

A research hub for the Principal Investigator.

Developed by Shashank Bangalore Lakshman

pi-hub is a local-first, installable web app that consolidates projects, team coordination, and academic deadlines into a single interface — built on the principle that nothing you type leaves your browser. No backend, no accounts, no telemetry. Just a fast, private notebook for running a research group.

  • Project lifecycle Exploration → planning → active → paused → archived, with logs and artifacts.
  • Team roster Members, roles, affiliations, expertise tags, and bidirectional project assignment.
  • Deadline countdowns Conferences with abstract, submission, notification, and event dates — live timers on the dashboard.
  • One-click PDF reports Browser-rendered reports for projects and contributors — no server round-trip.
  • JSON / ZIP backup Move data across devices manually — your only copy is the one you have, like a notebook.
  • Installable PWA Works fully offline after first load. Chrome, Edge, Safari (macOS + iOS), Firefox.

GemScan

Scam detection that never leaves your phone.

Developed by Shashank Bangalore Lakshman and Scott Eiers

GemScan is a fully private, on-device iOS app that screens suspicious messages, links, and screenshots for fraud — running a 4-bit quantized Gemma model entirely on the iPhone's Apple Silicon GPU via MLX-Swift. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is logged, and there are zero network calls during analysis. Median verdict: ~2.5s on an iPhone 15 Pro.

  • Text & link analysis Paste a message or URL and get a verdict with a confidence score and reasoning.
  • Screenshot detection Apple Vision OCR pulls text from images, then the classifier evaluates it.
  • Share extension Analyze straight from Messages, Mail, and Safari — no copy-paste needed.
  • Local history & stats Every check stored on-device, with monthly and yearly summaries.
  • Scam education A browsable guide to eight common patterns — phishing, romance, investment, and more.
  • Truly private Model downloads once, then runs offline — no analytics, no logs, no trackers.
