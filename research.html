The Confident Machine

When a model says it's sure — should you believe it?

The Story of Inference-Time Scaling

…and the training-time loop behind it.

Submitted to the 9th Workshop on Visualization for AI Explainability (VISxAI) at IEEE VIS 2026.

By Shashank Bangalore Lakshman and Scott Eiers

An explorable article that tells the story of inference-time scaling — how spending more compute at inference lets models reason further — and traces it back to the training-time loop that makes that scaling possible. A visual, step-through explainer of how the two phases feed each other.
